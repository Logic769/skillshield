# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking part in SkillShield a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being welcoming and patient, especially with people new to open source;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the people who use SkillShield.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission;
- demanding behaviour or a sense of entitlement towards maintainers' time;
- posting working malware, live malicious links or real secrets in project spaces;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the lead maintainer at **obielinda@gmail.com**. Every report will be reviewed and handled in confidence.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and may ban anyone from the project, temporarily or permanently, for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

## Enforcement steps

1. **Warning.** A private written warning that explains the problem.
2. **Temporary ban.** No interaction with the project for a set period.
3. **Permanent ban.** Removal from all project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.
